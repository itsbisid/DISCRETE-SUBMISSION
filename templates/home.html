About the Logic Reasoning Tutor

The Logic Reasoning Tutor is an interactive learning platform designed to help students strengthen their understanding of propositional logic, predicate logic, and foundational concepts in discrete mathematics. It combines guided lessons, automated feedback, and hands-on tools that make learning logical reasoning intuitive and engaging.

The system includes truth-table generation, formula classification, logical equivalence checking, and argument validity analysis—allowing users to explore and test logic expressions in real time. It also features a customizable quiz engine with 200 topic-tagged questions, hints, difficulty selection, and progress tracking.

With support for user accounts, guest mode, theme switching, and structured lessons, the app provides a modern and accessible way to master logic concepts for computer science, mathematics, and analytical thinking.
