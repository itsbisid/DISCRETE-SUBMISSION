Propositions & Connectives

A proposition is a statement that is either true or false. Logical connectives like ∧ (and), ∨ (or), ¬ (not), → (implies), and ↔ (if and only if) are used to build compound statements.

Truth Tables

Truth tables show the output of a logical formula for every possible combination of truth values of its variables.

Logical Equivalence

Two formulas are logically equivalent if they have the same truth value in every possible situation.

Tautology vs Contradiction

A tautology is always true; a contradiction is always false regardless of the truth values of its variables.
